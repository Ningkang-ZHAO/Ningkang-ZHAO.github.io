  • Master's thesis

  • 2022.09 - 2025.03
    Accident Tolerant Mechanism of Zirconium Alloy Fuel Cladding Based on Nanodiamond Coating

    Master's thesis on the high-temperature quenching and oxidation behavior of nanocrystalline-diamond-coated zirconium alloy cladding.

    • Designed and constructed high-temperature quenching and steam-oxidation experimental systems with temperature acquisition and high-speed visualization.
    • Characterized coating morphology and structural evolution using SEM/EDS, AFM, XPS, and Raman spectroscopy, with MATLAB-based image and transient-boiling analysis.
    • Demonstrated a 21.3% shorter film-boiling stage, a 15.06% higher boiling-transition temperature, and a 17.7% increase in peak cooling rate for NCD-coated zirconium alloy.
